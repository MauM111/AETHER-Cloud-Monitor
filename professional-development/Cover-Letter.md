# Cover Letter — Junior Cloud Engineer (Sample)

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in a Junior Cloud Engineer position. I am currently completing Level 5 of my Computing studies at Elizabeth School of London and developing practical skills in Linux administration, networking, Python programming and infrastructure monitoring.

As part of my academic studies, I developed **AETHER – Cloud Intelligence**, a Linux-based server monitoring application using Python, Flask and psutil. I configured an Ubuntu virtual machine and SSH access, developed a web dashboard, implemented resource alerts and CSV history, and configured a systemd service for automatic startup. This strengthened my understanding of Linux systems, troubleshooting and service management.

Alongside my studies, I have approximately ten years of experience as a Support Worker in Health and Social Care. This has strengthened my communication, teamwork, problem-solving and documentation skills, and my ability to remain organised when responding to unexpected situations. I am motivated to develop further in cloud infrastructure, automation and monitoring, and would welcome the opportunity to learn from experienced engineers.

Thank you for considering my application. I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss how my technical projects, academic studies and transferable professional skills could support your organisation.

Yours faithfully,  
Marius Morar
